1. Propane/GPL Storage — AFRIQUIAGAZ 22 m³

Placed — Conforme

Location: SE Corner

  • Single 22,000 L horizontal above-ground tank
  • Fenced enclosure with locked gate
  • ~20–25 m from property lines (south & east)
  • ~25 m to Chaufferie via LOCAL PLG
  • Tanker access from south entry, forward-only path

Why SE — Not Elsewhere

  • Art. 97: 7.5 m clearance satisfied in all directions
  • Art. 99: Truck loop through court → exit south (no reversing)
  • Art. 102: Open air, not under building
  • Away from admin (NE) and main entries
  • Short piping to chaufferie at south wall
Regulatory lock-in: Only the 12,000–24,000 L tier (Art. 97) fits this lot. Any capacity >24,000 L requires 10 m distances that encroach on B39-B40. A second 22 m³ tank may push total to >10 t — confirm with AFRIQUIAGAZ before ordering.

Premortem Risks

  • ⚠ 5.6-day autonomy is tight. A delivery delay during Ramadan, national holiday, or supplier truck breakdown could halt steam production within 48h of the reorder threshold.
  • ⚠ Tanker access conflict: GPL delivery trucks share the south entry with raw material trucks. Simultaneous arrivals could block the forward-only loop required by Art. 99.
  • ● Solar heating: Tank in full sun in Benguerir (40°C+ summers). Excess temperature raises tank pressure and increases vaporization losses. Needs shade structure or reflective paint.
  • ● Legend error: AFRIQUIAGAZ plan specifies 9 kg extinguisher but Art. 103 requires 10 kg minimum. Ensure 10 kg is actually installed.
Depends on: Chaufferie (steam user), Poste Transfo (safety electrical), LOCAL PLG (gas regulation), Fire fighting (RIA + extinguisher)

2. Chaufferie (Boiler House) — 2×4 t/h

Proposed — Verify

Optimal: South Wall Lean-to (SW)

  • 12 m × 8 m concrete slab — 96 m²
  • Lean-to against south face of west block
  • ~25 m from GPL, ~10 m from Water Park
  • Chimney 20 m pointed north (away from GPL)
  • Natural ventilation via south façade
  • Frees ~100 m² interior (old TGBT/Chaufferie zone)

PNV Position: Internal (Mid-West Block)

  • Interior location between TGBT and Maintenance
  • ~50 m GPL piping run
  • ~35 m from water treatment
  • Forced ventilation required (enclosed)
  • Chimney must traverse full roof height (18.5 m)
  • No crane/maintenance access from outside

Premortem Risks

  • ⚠ Setback violation: The south wall lean-to adds ~8 m beyond the building footprint. If the existing building is already at ~12 m from the south property line (B40), the chaufferie extension could encroach into the 10 m urbanistic setback. A surveyor must confirm before permit.
  • ⚠ PNV modification required: Moving the chaufferie from its PNV-approved internal position requires a formal modification request to the Prefecture. This could delay construction by 2–4 months if contested.
  • ⚠ Steam pipe length to wet line: From the SW position, steam must travel ~40 m to the east block (wet production). Heat loss, condensate accumulation, and water hammer risk increase with distance. Insulation and steam traps are critical.
  • ⚠ Chimney orientation: 20 m chimney pointing north sits above the production building. Prevailing winds from the Atlantic (W/NW) could push exhaust toward the east block or parking. Need wind rose analysis for Benguerir.
  • ● Foundation loads: Each boiler is 21.6 tonnes in service. The lean-to slab must be designed for this concentrated load — not a standard floor slab.
  • ✓ Gas piping halved: SW position cuts GPL piping from ~50 m to ~25 m — reducing cost, leak points, and pressure drop.
Depends on: GPL tank (fuel), Water Park (boiler feed), Poste Transfo (power), TGBT 2 (motor control), Dry & Wet lines (steam users)

3. Water Storage & Treatment — Parc d'Eau

Placed — Good

Location: SW Corner

  • Raw water tank: 250 m³ (3-day reserve)
  • Process water: 30 m³ (RO-treated)
  • Retort water: 25 m³ (for sterilization)
  • Treatment: RO + softener station
  • Private well + ONEE DN100 connection
  • ~10 m to Chaufferie (south position)

Why SW — Optimal Rationale

  • ONEE mains from south (DN100) → shortest connection
  • Private well on west boundary → direct intake
  • Adjacent to chaufferie (feed water = short run)
  • Gravity advantage: ground tanks feed pumps below building floor
  • Away from GPL (no contamination risk)

Premortem Risks

  • ⚠ Retort water distance: The 25 m³ retort tank at SW is ~60 m from the east block wet line (sterilization). Retort cooling requires rapid circulation of large water volumes. Long piping = slow response, higher pump energy, and temperature stratification. Consider a secondary retort tank near the wet line.
  • ⚠ Well yield unknown: Private well at Sidi Bouatmane — groundwater table depth and sustainable yield not confirmed. If the well runs dry in summer (Benguerir is semi-arid), the entire 250 m³ tank depends on ONEE alone, which has its own rationing history.
  • ⚠ 250 m³ tank foundation: A full 250 m³ tank weighs ~260 tonnes. The SW corner soil bearing capacity must be verified — Sidi Bouatmane is on the Rhamna plateau with variable geology.
  • ● Cross-contamination: Water park is ~20 m from GPL tank. While water doesn't contaminate gas, a GPL leak pooling downhill toward the water tanks creates a fire risk near the boiler feed. Verify grading drains away from the water park.
  • ✓ Boiler feed proximity: ~10 m to chaufferie south — minimizes heat loss in condensate return and softened water supply.
Depends on: Well (raw water), ONEE (backup supply), Chaufferie (feed water consumer), Wet line (retort/process water), STEP (return water treatment)

4. Electrical Distribution — Transfo + TGBT

Placed — Good

Poste Transfo + TGBT 2: South

  • HTA/BT transformer with 6 cells
  • TGBT 2 for BT distribution (south zone)
  • Adjacent to chaufferie (motor loads)
  • ONEE HTA feed from south boundary

TGBT 1: Internal West Block

  • Main distribution for dry production
  • Central to extruder/dryer motor loads
  • Existing PNV-approved position
  • Connected to Transfo via buried cable run

Premortem Risks

  • ⚠ Art. 101 conflict: Poste Transfo at south is ~15–20 m from the GPL tank. But Art. 101 requires all electrical equipment within 7.5 m to be safety-rated (ATEX). Verify the Transfo is outside the 7.5 m ATEX zone. If borderline, the Transfo needs ATEX-rated switchgear (significant cost).
  • ⚠ Cable route length to TGBT 1: TGBT 2 at south to TGBT 1 internal is ~30–40 m. For the heavy motor loads (extruder ~300 kW, dryer ~200 kW), voltage drop at this distance may require upsized cables (35 mm² → 70 mm²).
  • ⚠ Single transformer risk: One transformer serves the entire factory. Failure = total production stop. Consider whether a second transformer bay should be roughed in now (civil works cheaper during construction).
  • ● Flood risk at south position: Rainwater runoff flows downhill toward the south boundary. The transformer pit must be above grade or protected from water ingress.
Depends on: ONEE HTA feed, all production loads, Chaufferie (boiler controls), GPL (Art. 101 ATEX zone), Emergency generator

5. Compressed Air System

Not Yet Placed

A kibble + wet pet food plant requires compressed air (6–8 bar) for pneumatic conveyors, packaging machines, actuators, cleaning, and instrument air. Estimated demand: 15–25 m³/min at 7 bar.

Optimal: South Side of West Block (Interior)

  • Near the freed TGBT/Chaufferie space
  • Central to dry line (largest air consumer)
  • Short piping to packaging & pneumatic conveyors
  • Adjacent to maintenance shop for servicing
  • Noise contained within building envelope

Alternative: Chaufferie Lean-to Annex

  • Extend lean-to by ~4 m to house compressors
  • Natural ventilation (critical for air-cooled units)
  • Direct exhaust to exterior
  • Risk: longer piping run to production
  • Risk: adds load to lean-to foundation

Premortem Risks

  • ⚠ Forgotten system: Compressed air is not shown on the AFRIQUIAGAZ DWG or the current site plan. If space is not allocated now, it will be retrofitted into whatever corner remains — typically with undersized piping, poor ventilation, and excessive pressure drop.
  • ⚠ Heat rejection: Compressors reject ~80% of input energy as heat. In an enclosed room in Benguerir summer (40°C+), inlet temperature rises above rated spec (typically 40°C max), causing thermal shutdown. Ventilation or an air-cooled aftercooler is mandatory.
  • ⚠ Condensate drainage: Compressed air dryers produce condensate with trace oil. Must be connected to oil-water separator before draining to STEP, not to the fosse septique.
  • ● Noise: Screw compressors generate 75–85 dB(A). If placed near admin block (east), noise complaints from office staff. Interior west block location keeps noise within the production envelope.
Depends on: TGBT 1 (power ~100–200 kW), Maintenance shop (servicing), Dry line (main consumer), Wet line (secondary), STEP (condensate disposal)

6. Wastewater Treatment (STEP)

Not Placed — Critical

Pet food production generates high-BOD wastewater from meat preparation, cooking, CIP (clean-in-place), and retort cooling. Moroccan law (Loi 36-15 sur l'eau) requires on-site pre-treatment before discharge. Estimated effluent: 30–50 m³/day, BOD 2,000–5,000 mg/L.

Optimal: South Boundary, East of Water Park

  • Gravity flow from both production blocks (buildings higher)
  • Downhill toward south boundary
  • Near municipal sewer connection point
  • Accessible for sludge truck removal
  • Downwind of buildings (odor management)
  • Footprint: ~150–200 m² for DAF + equalization + aeration

Current: Only a Fosse Septique (SW Court)

  • Sized for domestic sewage only
  • Cannot handle industrial BOD loads
  • No fat/oil/grease separation
  • No equalization basin
  • Will overflow or breach permit within weeks of production start

Premortem Risks

  • ⚠ Regulatory showstopper: Loi 36-15 requires industrial pre-treatment. Without a STEP, the autorisation d'exploiter may be refused or revoked. Pet food effluent with BOD >1,000 mg/L cannot legally discharge to municipal sewer untreated.
  • ⚠ Grease trap missing: Wet line meat/oil waste will rapidly clog any downstream drainage. A DAF (dissolved air flotation) or at minimum a grease interceptor must be placed at the wet block drain manifold before the STEP.
  • ⚠ Space not reserved: A proper STEP for this effluent load requires 150–200 m². If not planned now, there may be no space left once the parking, GPL safety zone, and water park are built.
  • ⚠ Odor complaints: Aeration basins treating meat/fat waste produce hydrogen sulfide (H&sub2;S). Benguerir's prevailing winds are from the NW/W — a STEP at the south boundary pushes odor away from the factory and toward open land (good), but verify neighboring lots are not residential.
  • ⚠ Sludge disposal route: STEP produces 2–5 m³/week of sludge. Must have truck access for periodic removal. Locate near the south entry for tanker access.
Depends on: Wet line (primary effluent source), Dry line (CIP wash), Water Park (treated water for potential reuse), Fosse septique (domestic only), Municipal sewer connection

7. Emergency Generator (Groupe Électrogène)

Not Yet Placed

Benguerir industrial zone has intermittent ONEE outages (2–6 per month, typically 30 min–4 h). A diesel generator set (500–800 kVA) is essential for critical loads: cold rooms, boiler controls, lighting, fire pumps, sterilization autoclaves (batch cannot be interrupted).

Optimal: Adjacent to Poste Transfo (South)

  • Shortest cable run to ATS (automatic transfer switch)
  • Shared fuel storage with chaufferie area
  • Natural ventilation (exterior position)
  • Acoustic enclosure, exhaust pointing south (away from factory)
  • Diesel tank underground, ≤ 3,000 L (no special permit)

Alternative: NE Corner

  • Near admin block (UPS/critical IT loads)
  • Risk: far from main production loads
  • Risk: voltage drop over long cable run
  • Risk: noise disturbs office workers

Premortem Risks

  • ⚠ Retort batch loss: If power fails mid-sterilization cycle (120°C, 20–40 min), the entire batch (~2,500 cans) may be condemned. Without a generator, every ONEE outage during a retort cycle = direct product loss (15,000–30,000 MAD per batch).
  • ⚠ GPL ATEX zone: A diesel generator near the GPL tank creates an ignition source. Must be placed >7.5 m from the GPL tank (Art. 97/101). The south position near Transfo is ~15–20 m from GPL — safe, but verify.
  • ⚠ Cold chain continuity: Chambres positive/négative in the wet block store perishable raw materials. Without generator backup, 4 h of outage in summer = product spoilage (meat, not kibble).
  • ● Diesel supply chain: Separate fuel from propane logistics. Diesel tank must be ≥5 m from GPL per Moroccan ICPE regulations.
Depends on: Poste Transfo (ATS connection), TGBT 1 & 2 (load shedding logic), Cold rooms (critical load), Retort autoclaves (critical load), Fire pumps (life safety load)

8. Cooling Tower

Placed — Verify

Current: East Side, Near Wet Block

  • Adjacent to retort/sterilization area
  • Short cooling water loop
  • Open air position (good airflow)

Rationale

  • Retorts need rapid cooling (120°C → 40°C)
  • Proximity minimizes thermal loss in cooling loop
  • Make-up water from process tank (30 m³)

Premortem Risks

  • ⚠ Legionella risk: Cooling towers are regulated sources of Legionella in Morocco (Arrêté 2014). Located near the admin building air intakes (NE), aerosolized droplets could enter the HVAC system. Verify prevailing wind direction does not carry drift toward building air intakes.
  • ⚠ Water consumption: Evaporative cooling at Benguerir's ambient temperature (~35–40°C summer) uses 3–5 m³/h of makeup water. This is not accounted for in the Water Park sizing. Add ~60–80 m³/day to water demand calculations.
  • ● Chemical treatment drain: Blowdown water contains biocides and scale inhibitors. Must route to STEP, not to ground or fosse septique.
Depends on: Water Park (make-up water), Wet line retorts (heat load), STEP (blowdown disposal), TGBT (fan/pump power)

9. Fire Fighting System — Bâche + Surpresseur

Placed — Verify Coverage

Current: West Block Interior (Mid-South)

  • 35 m³ fire water reserve + booster pump
  • Local technique de lutte contre l'incendie
  • Feeds RIA (Robinet d'Incendie Armé) network

GPL Zone: Separate RIA + 10 kg Extinguisher

  • Art. 103: RIA + 10 kg powder at GPL tank
  • Water supply 1½" at 2 bar minimum
  • Connected to fire network via underground pipe

Premortem Risks

  • ⚠ 35 m³ may be insufficient: For a 10,900 m² industrial site with propane storage, Protection Civile typically requires 120 m³ (2 hours at 1,000 L/min). The current 35 m³ reserve provides only ~35 minutes. This will likely be flagged during the fire safety inspection.
  • ⚠ Pump power backup: Fire pumps must operate during a power failure (ONEE outage). Without a dedicated fire pump diesel driver or generator backup, the RIA network is dead during blackouts. NF EN 12845 requires an independent power source.
  • ⚠ East block coverage: The fire tank is in the west block. The east block (wet production + cold rooms) and admin are ~60–80 m away. Verify flow rate and pressure at the farthest RIA point — friction loss over 80 m of pipe may drop below the 2.5 bar minimum.
  • ● Fire truck access: Two south entries, but the lot has no perimeter fire access road. Protection Civile may require a 4 m wide access route around at least 3 sides of the building.
Depends on: Water Park (feed), Emergency generator (pump backup), GPL tank (Art. 103), All buildings (RIA coverage), Protection Civile approval

10. Fosse Septique (Sanitary)

Placed — Domestic Only

Currently placed at SW of the central truck court. Handles domestic sewage from admin toilets, cantine, and vestiaires only. This is correct — industrial wastewater must go to the STEP, not here.

Premortem Risks

  • ⚠ Cross-connection risk: During construction, it is common for floor drains in production areas to be accidentally connected to the fosse septique instead of the industrial drain manifold. A single mis-connection routes greasy, high-BOD wash water into the fosse → rapid saturation, overflow, groundwater contamination.
  • ● Sizing for staff growth: A pet food factory of this size may grow from ~80 to ~150 workers. Verify fosse is sized for peak staff count including canteen wastewater.
Depends on: Admin/cantine (sewage source), STEP (must NOT receive industrial waste)

Top 5 Risks — Priority Actions

1
No STEP planned. Industrial wastewater treatment is a legal requirement (Loi 36-15). Reserve 150–200 m² at the south boundary now. Commission a BOD/COD characterization study based on the production recipes.
2
Fire water reserve insufficient. 35 m³ provides ~35 min. Protection Civile likely requires 120 m³. Either upsize the bâche or designate the 250 m³ raw water tank as a dual-purpose fire reserve with a dedicated fire suction pipe.
3
No emergency generator. ONEE outages during retort cycles = batch loss. During cold room operation = spoilage. Size for critical loads (cold rooms + retort + fire pump + lighting), place adjacent to Transfo, verify ATEX clearance from GPL.
4
Chaufferie setback not surveyed. The lean-to south extension may encroach into the 10 m urbanistic setback. Commission a surveyor to confirm clearance before submitting the PNV modification.
5
Compressed air not allocated. Reserve space in the freed interior zone (ex-TGBT/Chaufferie) for 2–3 compressors, dryer, receiver, and adequate ventilation. Size the electrical feed at TGBT 1 now.

Recommended Utility Zoning

South Utility Corridor (the winning strategy): Cluster all major utilities along the south boundary of the lot, between the building south wall and the 10 m setback. This zone is ~101 m wide × ~15 m deep (~1,500 m²) and should contain, from west to east:

[Well] → [Water Park 250+30+25 m³] → [Chaufferie 12×8m] → [Transfo+TGBT2+PLG] → [Generator] → [STEP 150 m²] → [GPL 22 m³ + safety zone]

Benefits: All utilities accessible from the south for maintenance without entering production. All hazardous systems (GPL, boiler, generator) at maximum distance from occupied buildings. All piping runs between utilities are short (≤25 m). Truck access for GPL delivery, sludge removal, and diesel refill from the south entries. Single cable trench from Transfo to all motor loads.

Interior (freed zone): Compressed air + maintenance shop + fire pump room. Central to production, no regulatory distance requirements.

East side: Cooling tower only — must be close to retorts, positioned so drift blows away from admin building (prevailing W/NW wind pushes drift east, away from factory).
