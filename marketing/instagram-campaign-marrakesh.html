Bringing Premium Pet Nutrition to Route de Fès

An Instagram-first marketing campaign targeting affluent pet owners in Targa, Agdal, and the Route de Fès corridor — with automated workflows to turn followers into loyal customers.

Launch: September 2026 Duration: 8 weeks Primary channel: Instagram + WhatsApp Market: Marrakesh, Morocco
Target Audience

Who we're reaching

The Route de Fès corridor and neighboring Targa sit at the heart of Marrakesh's most affluent residential zone — villa communities, expat families, and young professionals who treat their pets as family.

🏠

Affluent Villa Owners

Targa & Route de Fès residents in villas (MAD 3.5M–8M+). Multiple pets, willing to pay for premium brands. Often have gardens — ideal for large breeds.

🌎

Expat Families

European and North American expats near international schools (Lycée Victor Hugo, ESCA). Accustomed to Royal Canin, Hill's, Orijen. Seek trusted local suppliers with home delivery.

📱

Young Professionals

Guéliz & Agdal millennials and Gen-Z. Instagram-native, follow pet influencers, treat pets as lifestyle statements. Value aesthetics, sustainability, and convenience.

Instagram Strategy

Four content pillars

Every post maps to one of four pillars. This keeps the feed coherent and ensures we rotate between education, personality, proof, and promotion.

01

Educate — "Le Saviez-Vous?"

Nutrition tips, ingredient breakdowns, breed-specific feeding guides. Position PetFactory as the trusted expert, not just a shop. Carousel format works best — save-worthy, shareable content that the algorithm rewards.

Carousels FR + AR captions 2×/week
02

Personality — "Nos Amis Route de Fès"

Customer pet spotlights, behind-the-scenes at the store, team introductions. Reels showing real pets reacting to food, neighborhood dogs on walks past the shop. Authenticity over polish — phone-shot Reels outperform studio content.

Reels UGC reposts 3×/week
03

Social Proof — "Témoignages"

Customer reviews, before/after coat transformations, vet endorsements. Screenshot WhatsApp praise (with permission). Testimonials in Darija build local trust faster than polished French copy.

Stories Static posts 1×/week
04

Promote — "Offres PetFactory"

New arrivals, weekly specials, loyalty rewards, WhatsApp-exclusive deals. Always pair a promotion with a clear CTA: "DM us" or "Click the WhatsApp link in bio." Morocco's purchase decisions close on WhatsApp, not on checkout pages.

Stories + Feed WhatsApp CTA 2×/week
